## Overview

DevReady is an online interview practice platform built for IT students and junior developers. The product focuses on helping learners prepare for technical interviews through structured question sets, topic-based learning paths, personal progress tracking, and AI-assisted practice.

The goal of the project is to reduce the gap between classroom knowledge and real interview expectations by giving learners a focused environment to review, practice, and improve consistently.

## Key Features

- **Question Library**: Browse interview questions by technical topics and open detailed explanations for deeper review.
- **ReadyBot**: Practice with an AI assistant designed to support interview preparation and technical self-study.
- **User Dashboard**: Track learning activity, progress, and saved practice content in one place.
- **Profile Management**: Manage personal account information and learning-related settings.
- **Responsive Interface**: Provide a clean experience across desktop and mobile screens.

## My Role

I worked on the frontend experience for DevReady, focusing on translating product requirements into usable screens, building responsive interfaces, and making the learning flow clear for students preparing for interviews.
