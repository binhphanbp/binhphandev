## Overview

Serenity is a mobile spa booking app UI/UX design created in Figma for the 48h Figma HackDesign challenge. The project explores a calm and polished booking experience where users can discover spa services, view details, and move through a reservation flow with confidence.

The design focuses on a clean mobile-first experience, soft visual hierarchy, and simple actions that match the expectations of a beauty and wellness service app.

## Key Features

- **Service Discovery**: Mobile screens designed to help users browse spa services and understand available options quickly.
- **Booking Flow**: A focused reservation experience for selecting services and moving toward appointment booking.
- **Beauty & Wellness Visual Direction**: A soft, elegant interface style aligned with spa and self-care products.
- **Mobile App Structure**: Screen layouts prepared around common mobile usage patterns and touch-friendly interaction.

## My Role

I designed the mobile app experience in Figma, including visual direction, screen layout, interaction flow, and overall UI composition for the Serenity spa booking concept.

- **Figma**: Used for UI/UX design, screen composition, prototyping direction, and final presentation.
